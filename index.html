05 / Before we start

Good questions.
Clear answers.

How much does a game cost?

Every game gets a personalized quote based on its features, complexity and scope. Send us your idea and we’ll agree on a price before work begins.

Can you build my type of game?

Yes. We create all types of Roblox games, from simulators and tycoons to obbies, horror, RPGs and original concepts. Tell us what you have in mind.

When does the 3–7 day timeline start?

Production starts once the brief and quote are approved. We confirm the delivery date for your project before starting; larger scopes may need a longer timeline.

What should I send you?

Your game idea, the main features and any references. If you already have a budget or a deadline, include those too. A full design document isn’t required.
